Create a clean, visually appealing **16:9 landscape** educational one-pager in a handwritten-notebook style.

**BRANDING & LAYOUT:**
- Background: white
- TOP RIGHT corner: small rounded badge — deep blue (#1B4FD8) background, white "OlympiadReady" text
- Layout: grid of hand-drawn shape cards, 3 rows × 4 columns = 12 shapes
- Each card has: hand-drawn shape, formula in blue, a mini labeled diagram
- Bottom center: "Crack Every Olympiad" tagline

**TITLE:**
"Area & Perimeter — Every Shape, One Page" ✏️

**SHAPES GRID (12 cards):**

1. **Square** — Area = s² | Perimeter = 4s
2. **Rectangle** — Area = l×b | Perimeter = 2(l+b)
3. **Triangle** — Area = ½×b×h | Perimeter = a+b+c (draw labeled scalene)
4. **Right Triangle** — Area = ½×base×height | draw with legs labeled
5. **Equilateral Triangle** — Area = (√3/4)a² | Perimeter = 3a (★ highlight)
6. **Circle** — Area = πr² | Circumference = 2πr (draw r and d labeled)
7. **Semi-circle** — Area = πr²/2 | Perimeter = πr + 2r
8. **Parallelogram** — Area = base × height | Perimeter = 2(a+b)
9. **Trapezium** — Area = ½(a+b)×h | Perimeter = sum of all sides (draw with parallel sides a,b)
10. **Rhombus** — Area = ½×d₁×d₂ | Perimeter = 4a (diagonals labeled)
11. **Kite** — Area = ½×d₁×d₂ | Perimeter = 2(a+b)
12. **Sector** — Area = (θ/360)×πr² | Arc = (θ/360)×2πr

**Bottom strip (yellow highlight) — "Don't Mix These Up!":**
- "Perimeter = BOUNDARY (add all sides)"
- "Area = SURFACE inside (always in square units)"
- "Height is PERPENDICULAR — never the slant side!"
- "π ≈ 22/7 or 3.14 — use 22/7 for cleaner MCQ answers"
