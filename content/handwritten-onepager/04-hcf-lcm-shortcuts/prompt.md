**LEFT COLUMN — HCF (Highest Common Factor):**
Subtitle: "GCD — Biggest number that divides both"

Methods (with mini worked examples):
1. Prime Factorisation Method — take LOWEST power of common primes
   - HCF(36, 48) → 36=2²×3², 48=2⁴×3 → HCF = 2²×3 = 12
2. Division Method (Euclidean Algorithm)
   - HCF(48,36): 48÷36=rem12 → 36÷12=rem0 → HCF=12

Quick rules box:
- HCF of co-primes = 1
- HCF ≤ smaller number
- HCF × LCM = Product of two numbers (★)

**RIGHT COLUMN — LCM (Lowest Common Multiple):**
Subtitle: "Smallest number both can divide into"

Methods:
1. Prime Factorisation — take HIGHEST power of all primes
   - LCM(12,18) → 12=2²×3, 18=2×3² → LCM = 2²×3² = 36
2. Division Ladder Method (L-shape division)
   - Show L-shape: divide by 2,3 etc. until all 1s, multiply divisors

Quick rules box:
- LCM ≥ larger number
- LCM of co-primes = their product
- Use: LCM = (a×b) ÷ HCF (★)
