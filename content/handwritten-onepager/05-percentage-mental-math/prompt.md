**TITLE:**
"% Percentage Tricks — Calculate in Your Head!" ✏️

**SECTION 1 — "The Reversal Trick" (top left box, blue border):**
"x% of y = y% of x"
- 8% of 25 = 25% of 8 = 2 ← so much easier!
- 4% of 75 = 75% of 4 = 3
- "Use this when one number is 'friendlier'"

**SECTION 2 — "The Building Block Method" (top right box, green border):**
10% = divide by 10
5% = half of 10%
1% = divide by 100
20% = 2 × 10%
15% = 10% + 5%
25% = divide by 4
50% = divide by 2
75% = 50% + 25%

Example: 35% of 80
→ 10% = 8, so 30% = 24, 5% = 4
→ 35% = 28 ✓

**SECTION 3 — "Successive Percentage Change" (middle strip, orange background):**
If price increases by a% then b%:
Net change = a + b + (a×b)/100
Example: 20% up then 25% up = 20+25+5 = 50% up ✓
"Works for discounts too — use negatives!"

**SECTION 4 — "Fraction ↔ Percentage Conversion" (bottom left, hand-drawn table):**
1/2 = 50% | 1/3 = 33.3% | 1/4 = 25% | 1/5 = 20%
1/6 = 16.7% | 1/7 ≈ 14.3% | 1/8 = 12.5% | 1/9 ≈ 11.1%
1/10 = 10% | 1/11 ≈ 9.1% | 1/12 ≈ 8.3% | 2/3 = 66.7%

**SECTION 5 — "Olympiad Traps" (bottom right, red warning box):**
⚠ "% increase ≠ % decrease reversal"
- 50% up then 50% down = 25% loss (NOT zero!)
⚠ "% of what? Always check the base!"
