# Prompt: Speed, Distance & Time — Complete Cheat Sheet

**Use in:** ChatGPT (GPT-4o image generation) or DALL·E 3

---

Create a clean, visually appealing **16:9 landscape** educational one-pager in a handwritten-notebook style.

**BRANDING & LAYOUT:**
- Background: white
- TOP RIGHT corner: small rounded badge — deep blue (#1B4FD8) background, white "OlympiadReady" text
- Central formula triangle (DST triangle) as visual anchor
- 5 scenario sections around it
- Bottom center: "Crack Every Olympiad" tagline

**TITLE:**
"Speed, Distance, Time — Every Formula You'll Need" 🚂 ✏️

**CENTRAL ELEMENT — The SDT Triangle:**
Large hand-drawn triangle (like the famous physics triangle):
- Top: D (Distance)
- Bottom-left: S (Speed)
- Bottom-right: T (Time)
- Cover D → S × T | Cover S → D/T | Cover T → D/S
Label outside: "Cover what you want to find!"

**SECTION 1 (top left) — "Core Formulas":**
- Speed = Distance ÷ Time
- Distance = Speed × Time
- Time = Distance ÷ Speed
- "Units: km/hr or m/s — always check!"
- Conversion: km/hr to m/s → multiply by 5/18
- m/s to km/hr → multiply by 18/5

**SECTION 2 (top right) — "Average Speed":**
- Going and coming back (same distance):
  Average Speed = 2ab/(a+b) where a,b are the two speeds
  NOT the simple average (a+b)/2 !!
- Example: 60 km/hr there, 40 km/hr back
  → Avg = 2×60×40/(60+40) = 4800/100 = 48 km/hr

**SECTION 3 (middle left) — "Relative Speed":**
- Same direction: Relative Speed = |S₁ − S₂|
- Opposite direction: Relative Speed = S₁ + S₂
- "Time for faster to overtake slower = Gap ÷ Relative Speed"

**SECTION 4 (middle right) — "Train Problems":**
- Train crossing a pole/person: Distance = Length of train
- Train crossing a platform: Distance = Length of train + Length of platform
- Two trains crossing each other:
  - Opposite direction: Distance = L₁+L₂, Speed = S₁+S₂
  - Same direction: Distance = L₁+L₂, Speed = |S₁−S₂|

**SECTION 5 (bottom) — "Time & Distance Word Problem Types":**
Mini worked example for each:
1. Meeting problem: "A and B start from P and Q, 120km apart..."
   → Time to meet = Total distance ÷ Sum of speeds
2. Early/Late problems: "If I walk at 4km/h, I'm 10 min late; at 5km/h, 10 min early..."
   → Set up equations using D = S×T

**Bottom strip (yellow):**
"★ Most common MCQ trap: Average speed ≠ (S1+S2)/2 when distances are EQUAL"
"★ Convert ALL units before solving — mixed units = wrong answer guaranteed"
