**TITLE:**
"States of Matter — Solid, Liquid, Gas & Plasma" 🧊🔥 ✏️

**CENTRAL DIAGRAM — "The State Cycle":**
Circle with 4 states at corners (or a cycle diagram):
- SOLID (blue) → LIQUID (green) → GAS (orange) → PLASMA (red)
Arrows between each state labeled with the transition name:
- Solid → Liquid: Melting (add heat)
- Liquid → Gas: Vaporisation / Boiling (add heat)
- Gas → Liquid: Condensation (remove heat)
- Liquid → Solid: Freezing (remove heat)
- Solid → Gas directly: Sublimation (e.g., dry ice, camphor)
- Gas → Solid directly: Deposition
- Ionise beyond gas = PLASMA (lightning, sun)

**LEFT PANEL — "Properties Comparison" table:**

| Property | Solid | Liquid | Gas |
|-----------|-------|--------|-----|
| Shape | Fixed | Takes container shape | No fixed shape |
| Volume | Fixed | Fixed | No fixed volume |
| Particle arrangement | Tightly packed | Close, can move | Far apart, random |
| Compressibility | Very low | Very low | High |
| Diffusion | Very slow | Slow | Fast |
| Examples | Ice, rock | Water, oil | Steam, air |

**RIGHT PANEL — "Real Examples + Olympiad Facts":**

- "Exception: Ice is less dense than water (that's why ice floats!)"
- "Brownian Motion: random movement of particles in liquid/gas (proves particles exist)"

**Bottom strip (yellow):**
"REMEMBER: Adding heat → particles gain energy → bonds break → state changes"
