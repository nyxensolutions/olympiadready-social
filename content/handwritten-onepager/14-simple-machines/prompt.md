**TITLE:**
"Simple Machines — 6 Types, Every Formula" ⚙️ ✏️

**6 MACHINE CARDS:**

**Card 1 — Lever** (blue tint)
- Diagram: seesaw/lever with fulcrum, effort arm (E), load arm (L)
- Formula: Load × Load arm = Effort × Effort arm
- MA (Mechanical Advantage) = Load/Effort = Effort arm/Load arm
- Class 1 example: scissors, seesaw | Class 2: wheelbarrow | Class 3: tweezers
- Classes diagram: show fulcrum position for Class 1/2/3

**Card 2 — Wheel & Axle** (green tint)
- Diagram: steering wheel with small axle
- Formula: MA = Radius of wheel / Radius of axle
- Examples: steering wheel, doorknob, screwdriver

**Card 3 — Pulley** (orange tint)
- Diagram: fixed pulley vs movable pulley
- Fixed pulley: MA = 1 (changes direction only)
- Movable pulley: MA = 2
- Block & tackle: MA = number of rope segments supporting load
- Example: flagpole, crane, well bucket

**Card 4 — Inclined Plane / Ramp** (purple tint)
- Diagram: ramp with length L, height H
- Formula: MA = Length of slope / Height = L/H
- Less steep = more mechanical advantage
- Examples: ramp, road on hillside, chute

**Card 5 — Wedge** (red tint)
- Diagram: wedge shape (two inclined planes back to back)
- Formula: MA = Length / Width at thick end
- Examples: knife, axe, needle, nail, chisel

**Card 6 — Screw** (teal tint)
- Diagram: screw thread showing pitch
- Formula: MA = 2πL / Pitch (L = length of screwdriver handle)
- Pitch = distance between threads
- Examples: screw, bolt, jar lid, drill

**Bottom strip (yellow):**
"KEY CONCEPT: Simple machines don't reduce WORK — they reduce EFFORT by increasing distance"
"Work = Force × Distance | Input work = Output work (ideal machine)"
"MA > 1 means force multiplied | VR = Velocity Ratio | Efficiency = (MA/VR) × 100%"
