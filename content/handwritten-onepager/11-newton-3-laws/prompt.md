# Prompt: Newton's 3 Laws of Motion

**Use in:** ChatGPT (GPT-4o image generation) or DALL·E 3

---

Create a clean, visually appealing **16:9 landscape** educational one-pager in a handwritten-notebook style.

**BRANDING & LAYOUT:**
- Background: white
- TOP RIGHT corner: small rounded badge — deep blue (#1B4FD8) background, white "OlympiadReady" text
- 3 large horizontal strips (one per law), each with its own accent colour
- Hand-drawn illustrations inside each strip (no clipart — sketch style)
- Bottom center: "Crack Every Olympiad" tagline

**TITLE:**
"Newton's 3 Laws of Motion — With Real-Life Examples" ⚡ ✏️

**STRIP 1 — First Law (Inertia)** (blue background, light tint):
- Large "1" in corner
- Law text: "An object at rest stays at rest; an object in motion stays in motion — unless acted on by an external force."
- Key word highlighted: "INERTIA = laziness of objects"
- Formula: F_net = 0 → no change in velocity
- Hand-drawn examples (3 sketches in a row):
  1. Person sliding forward when bus brakes suddenly
  2. Dust falling off a shaken carpet
  3. Ball rolling forever on frictionless surface (with dotted arrow)
- Olympiad note: "If no net force → velocity doesn't change (can still be moving!)"

**STRIP 2 — Second Law (F = ma)** (green background, light tint):
- Large "2" in corner
- Law text: "The force on an object equals its mass times its acceleration."
- Big formula: **F = m × a** | Units: Newton (N) = kg·m/s²
- Rearrangements: a = F/m | m = F/a
- Hand-drawn examples:
  1. Pushing an empty vs full shopping cart (less mass → more acceleration)
  2. Rocket launch (huge F → huge a)
- Key insight: "Same force, double the mass → half the acceleration"

**STRIP 3 — Third Law (Action-Reaction)** (orange background, light tint):
- Large "3" in corner
- Law text: "For every action, there is an equal and opposite reaction."
- Hand-drawn examples:
  1. Rocket exhaust going down, rocket going up (arrows opposite)
  2. Swimmer pushing water back, moves forward
  3. Gun recoil — bullet forward, gun pushes back
- Key insight: Action & reaction act on DIFFERENT objects (they don't cancel!)

**Bottom strip (yellow):**
"COMMON TRAP: 3rd Law pairs act on different objects. They cannot cancel each other!"
"Olympiad mnemonics: 1=Inertia | 2=F=ma | 3=Every action has reaction"

**Art direction:** Each strip feels distinct — use the three accent colours for the law number, heading, and illustrations. The physics diagrams use arrows to show forces (red arrows = force, blue = direction of motion). Clean, textbook-style diagrams in a handwritten aesthetic.
