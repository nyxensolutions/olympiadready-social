**LEFT PANEL — "Inputs" (pink/red tint box):**

- Chlorophyll → found in chloroplasts, in mesophyll cells

**RIGHT PANEL — "Outputs" (green tint box):**
- Glucose (C₆H₁₂O₆) → stored as starch, used for energy
- Oxygen (O₂) → released through stomata → we breathe this!

**Bottom strip (yellow):**
"WHERE: Chloroplasts (specifically the thylakoid for light reactions, stroma for dark reactions)"
"WHY LEAVES ARE GREEN: Chlorophyll absorbs red and blue light, reflects green"
"OLYMPIAD TRAP: Photosynthesis stores energy. Respiration releases it. Both happen in plants!"
