# Prompt: Blood Relations Shortcut — Family Tree Method

**Use in:** ChatGPT (GPT-4o image generation) or DALL·E 3

---

Create a clean, visually appealing **16:9 landscape** educational one-pager in a handwritten-notebook style.

**BRANDING & LAYOUT:**
- Background: cream white
- TOP RIGHT corner: small rounded badge — deep blue (#1B4FD8) background, white "OlympiadReady" text
- Central family tree diagram with branches — the visual anchor
- Formula/relation key on the sides
- Bottom center: "Crack Every Olympiad" tagline

**TITLE:**
"Blood Relations — Draw the Tree, Solve in 10 Seconds" 🌳 ✏️

**MAIN DIAGRAM (center) — Hand-drawn family tree:**
```
          [Grandfather] ── [Grandmother]
                  │
    ┌─────────────┼─────────────┐
  [Uncle]      [Father] ──── [Mother]    [Aunt]
                       │
          ┌────────────┼────────────┐
       [Elder    ]   [ME]      [Younger
        Brother]                Sister]
           │
        [Nephew/
         Niece]
```
Use circles (○) for female, squares (□) for male, horizontal line for marriage, vertical for child.
Legend in corner: ○ = Female, □ = Male, ─── = Married, │ = Child of

**RIGHT SIDE — "Relation Quick Reference" table:**

| Relation | Who they are |
|----------|-------------|
| Maternal uncle | Mother's brother |
| Paternal aunt | Father's sister |
| Cousin | Uncle/Aunt's child |
| Nephew | Brother's/Sister's son |
| Niece | Brother's/Sister's daughter |
| Sibling-in-law | Spouse's brother/sister |
| Grand-nephew | Nephew's/Niece's child |

**LEFT SIDE — "Tricky Clue Words":**
- "only child" → no siblings
- "only son/daughter" → specify gender
- "son-in-law" → daughter's husband
- "daughter-in-law" → son's wife
- "step-" → not blood related, from remarriage

**Bottom strip (yellow):**
"METHOD: Read question → Draw boxes/circles → Connect with lines → Re-read from the tree"
"TRAP: 'A is the father of B's son' → B's son's father = A, so A is B's husband (not her father!)"

---
**Art direction:** The family tree is the star — make it large, clear, occupying center of the page. Different generations on different levels. Small person icons (stick figures or simple heads) in each node. The tree lines look hand-drawn with a pencil. Two narrower panels left and right for the table and tips.
